ABOUT THE URBAN CANVAS

THE URBAN CANVAS is a multidisciplinary Architecture, Planning, Construction & Design Studio based in Agra, Uttar Pradesh. Founded by Ar. Aman Sharma, the studio delivers technically sound, context-driven and premium design solutions across residential, commercial, healthcare, industrial, institutional and urban development projects. Every project is approached with a balance of functionality, innovation and timeless architectural thinking.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

We believe architecture should go beyond aesthetics. Every project should respond to its context, solve real problems and create lasting value. Through thoughtful planning, technical excellence and attention to detail, we strive to design spaces that remain functional, timeless and relevant for generations.

OUR VISION

To establish THE URBAN CANVAS as one of Agra's most respected multidisciplinary architecture and design studios, recognised for delivering technically strong, innovative and premium architectural solutions across projects of every scale.
