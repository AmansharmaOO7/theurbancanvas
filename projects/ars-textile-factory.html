ARS TEXTILE FACTORY

Contemporary Industrial Facade

THE PROJECT

A Contemporary Industrial Identity

ARS Textile Factory is a G+2 factory and office project located in Runakta, Agra, designed by THE URBAN CANVAS.

Our scope for the project focused on the front elevation, with the objective of creating a contemporary architectural identity for the factory and office building.

The facade combines modern industrial aesthetics with a contemporary material palette to establish a strong and refined frontage.
