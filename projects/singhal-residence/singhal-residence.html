SINGHAL RESIDENCE

Modern Urban Residential Architecture

THE PROJECT

A Balance of Openness, Privacy & Modernity

Singhal Residence is a contemporary G+1 residential project located in Dayal Bagh, developed with a focus on balancing openness, privacy, and a modern urban aesthetic.

THE URBAN CANVAS was involved in the architecture and 3D visualization of the residence, translating the design intent into detailed architectural drawings and visualizations.

The architectural expression combines clean modern geometry with vertical architectural fins and perforated ceiling detailing to create a distinctive residential character.

LOCATION Dayal Bagh
CLIENT Shekhar Singhal
TYPOLOGY Residential
CONFIGURATION G+1
SITE / BUILT-UP AREA 105.57 sqm / 163.65 sq m
OUR SCOPE Architecture & 3D Visualization
DESIGN CHARACTER Openness, Privacy & Modern Urban Aesthetic
KEY ELEMENT Vertical Architectural Fins
PROJECT DATA

Architectural Planning & Detailing

The Singhal Residence is planned as a G+1 home with an architectural approach that balances openness and privacy within a contemporary urban setting.

Vertical architectural fins form a key element of the facade, creating visual rhythm while contributing to the modern architectural identity of the residence.

Perforated ceiling detailing adds another layer of architectural expression, complementing the overall contemporary character of the house.
