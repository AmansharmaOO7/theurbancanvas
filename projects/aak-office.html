AAK OFFICE

Contemporary Office Front Elevation

THE PROJECT

A Contemporary Office Facade

AAK Office is a contemporary G+2 office building located in Kamla Nagar, Agra, designed by THE URBAN CANVAS.

Our scope for the project focused on the front elevation, developing a contemporary architectural identity for the office facade.

The design uses vertical facade articulation together with a carefully composed rhythm of double-height glazing to create depth and visual emphasis across the frontage.

LOCATION Kamla Nagar, Agra
CLIENT Parth Agarwal
TYPOLOGY Office
CONFIGURATION G+2
PROJECT DATA

Scale & Design Scope

The project occupies a compact 19' × 32.5' plot. THE URBAN CANVAS was commissioned for the front elevation design of the G+2 office building.

PLOT SIZE 19' × 32.5'
SCOPE Front Elevation Design
DESIGN STYLE Contemporary / Modern
KEY ELEMENT Vertical Articulation

DESIGN APPROACH

Vertical Rhythm & Light

The facade is composed around a strong vertical rhythm, giving the relatively narrow frontage a more defined architectural presence.

Double-height glazing introduces visual openness while establishing a contrasting rhythm within the vertical facade composition.
