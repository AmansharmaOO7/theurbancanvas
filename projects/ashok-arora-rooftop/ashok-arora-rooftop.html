THE PROJECT

Contemporary Outdoor Living

The Residential Rooftop project at ADA Housing transforms an existing rooftop into a contemporary outdoor living environment designed for relaxation, entertainment and social interaction.

THE URBAN CANVAS was involved in the designing and visualization of the rooftop, developing a contemporary environment with integrated entertainment facilities and comfortable outdoor seating.

A pergola-covered seating area and an integrated bar and entertainment space form the key elements of the rooftop experience.

LOCATION ADA Housing
CLIENT Ashok Arora
TYPOLOGY Rooftop Design
CONFIGURATION NA
AREA 91.54 sq m
OUR SCOPE Designing & Visualization
DESIGN CHARACTER Contemporary Outdoor Living
KEY ELEMENT Integrated Bar & Pergola Seating
PROJECT DATA

Designed for Outdoor Living

The rooftop is conceived as an extension of the residential living experience, creating a dedicated environment for leisure and social interaction.

The integrated bar and entertainment space provides a focal point for gatherings, while the pergola-covered seating creates a shaded and comfortable outdoor environment.

DESIGN APPROACH

Contemporary Outdoor Living

The design focuses on creating a seamless outdoor living experience within the available rooftop area. Functional zones are integrated into a cohesive contemporary setting.

The bar and entertainment area provides a dedicated social space, while the pergola-covered seating introduces shade and comfort for everyday use.

The overall composition combines functionality, contemporary aesthetics and an informal atmosphere suited to rooftop living.
