HMEL PARK

Community-Centric Landscape Development

THE PROJECT

A Landscape Designed for Community

HMEL Park is a one-hectare community-centric landscape development located in Sehe Kalan, Rajasthan.

THE URBAN CANVAS was responsible for the integrated landscape planning and design of the park, bringing together recreational, sporting and community-oriented open spaces.

The design draws from the architectural character of Rajasthan while creating a diverse landscape environment for recreation, gathering and everyday community use.

LOCATION Sehe Kalan, Rajasthan
CLIENT HMEL
TYPOLOGY Landscape Development
SITE AREA 1 Hectare
DESIGN SCOPE

Integrated Landscape Planning

The project involved integrated landscape planning and designing across the one-hectare site, with emphasis on creating connected recreational and community spaces.

The landscape programme brings together active recreation, informal gathering, pedestrian movement and landscaped open spaces within a unified site design.

DESIGN APPROACH

Landscape, Recreation & Community

The landscape design is conceived as a community-oriented open-space system where recreation, movement and social interaction coexist within a connected green environment.

Recreational and sports facilities are integrated with landscaped greens, walking paths, seating areas and gathering spaces to create a varied experience across the site.

Elements inspired by Rajasthani architecture contribute to the project's regional identity while supporting the functional requirements of a contemporary community landscape.
