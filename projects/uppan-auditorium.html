INSTITUTIONAL ARCHITECTURE

UPPAN AUDITORIUM

Modern Institutional Auditorium

THE PROJECT

Visualising a Modern Institutional Landmark

The Uppan Auditorium is a G+1 institutional auditorium with a double-height internal volume, located at Police Modern School, Ghaziabad.

THE URBAN CANVAS was engaged for 3D visualization and walkthrough development, translating the proposed architectural concept into a detailed visual presentation.

The visual language follows a modern institutional expression, with emphasis on vertical facade articulation and the integration of natural light.

LOCATION Police Modern School, Ghaziabad
CLIENT Uttar Pradesh Police Awas Nigam
PROJECT TYPE Institutional Auditorium
CONFIGURATION G+1
AUDITORIUM Double Height
OUR SCOPE 3D Visualization & Walkthrough
DESIGN CONCEPT

Modern Institutional Expression

The architectural expression is conceived with a modern institutional character, creating a strong and recognisable identity appropriate for an educational campus.

Vertical facade articulation provides visual rhythm to the building while helping establish a sense of scale and proportion across the elevation.

Natural light integration forms another important component of the design, contributing to the visual quality of the institutional environment.
