THE PROJECT

Contemporary Industrial Reception

The ARS Factory Reception Area is a contemporary interior design project located in Runakta, Agra, created for Ashok Arora.

THE URBAN CANVAS was involved in the Interior Design & 3D Visualization of the reception space, developing a modern corporate aesthetic within a compact 5m × 4.5m reception area.

A key element of the design is the double-height spatial experience, giving the reception a stronger sense of volume and creating a contemporary arrival space.

LOCATION Runakta, Agra
CLIENT Ashok Arora
TYPOLOGY Industrial Interior
CONFIGURATION Reception Area
AREA 5m × 4.5m
OUR SCOPE Interior Design & 3D Visualization
DESIGN CHARACTER Contemporary Reception Space
KEY ELEMENT Double-Height Spatial Experience
PROJECT DATA

Modern Corporate Aesthetics

The reception area is designed with a contemporary interior language that establishes a modern corporate identity for the industrial facility.

The double-height spatial experience forms the primary architectural feature of the reception, enhancing the sense of openness and volume within the entrance space.

Contemporary Industrial Interior

Through interior design and 3D visualization, THE URBAN CANVAS developed the proposed reception environment into a detailed visual presentation.
