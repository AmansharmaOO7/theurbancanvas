RESIDENTIAL ARCHITECTURE

CHAYYA UPADHAYA RESIDENCE

Contemporary Living | Agra, Uttar Pradesh

THE PROJECT

Contemporary Residential Architecture

Chayya Upadhaya Residence is a contemporary G+1 residential project located in Agra, Uttar Pradesh. The design focuses on a balanced facade composition, bringing together material contrast, vertical glazing and carefully considered architectural details.

THE URBAN CANVAS was responsible for Architectural Design and Elevation Design, developing a cohesive residential expression that balances contemporary aesthetics with the functional requirements of a home.

A stone-textured feature wall, vertical glazing and timber accents establish the key visual elements of the residence, giving the facade depth, warmth and a distinctive modern character.

LOCATION Agra, Uttar Pradesh
CLIENT Chayya Upadhaya
TYPOLOGY Residential
CONFIGURATION Ground + 1 Floor
SITE AREA 1,000 sq ft
BUILT-UP AREA 2,200 sq ft
OUR SCOPE Architectural & Elevation Design
DESIGN CHARACTER Contemporary Residence
DESIGN APPROACH

Material, Light and Proportion

The architectural expression is developed around a balanced arrangement of solid surfaces, glazing and accent materials. The stone-textured feature wall provides a visual anchor, while vertical glazing introduces openness and a contemporary character.

Timber accents add warmth and contrast to the facade. Together, these elements create a layered composition in which material textures, vertical elements and openings contribute to the overall architectural identity.
