RESIDENTIAL ARCHITECTURE

RK GUPTA RESIDENCE

Contemporary Residential Architecture

THE PROJECT

Contemporary Architecture with a Modern Identity

RK Gupta Residence is a G+2 residential project located in Agra, developed as a contemporary mixed-use development.

THE URBAN CANVAS was responsible for the architectural planning and design of the project, including the development of its architectural elevations.

The design explores modern aesthetics through a contemporary architectural expression while responding to the requirements of the site and building programme.

LOCATION Agra
CLIENT RK Gupta
CONFIGURATION G+2
PLOT SIZE 29.5' × 59'
BUILT-UP AREA 4,350 sq ft
SCOPE Architectural Planning & Design
ARCHITECTURAL PLANNING

Planning & Spatial Development

The project involved architectural planning and design for the G+2 development, establishing the spatial framework for the residence and its overall architectural expression.

The planning is supported by rendered plan studies and technical documentation developed as part of the architectural design process.
