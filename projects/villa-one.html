VILLA ONE

Contemporary Luxury Residence

THE PROJECT

Contemporary Luxury, Crafted for Living

Villa One is a contemporary luxury residence designed by THE URBAN CANVAS in Dayal Bagh, Agra.

The G+1 residence combines a clean contemporary architectural language with carefully composed elements that give the home a distinctive identity.

A double-height staircase forms an important spatial element of the residence, while floating balconies and a perforated terrace roof introduce depth, light and visual character to the elevation.

DESIGN APPROACH

Contemporary Architecture

The architectural composition uses layered volumes and projecting balconies to create depth across the facade.

The double-height staircase adds a strong vertical spatial experience, while the perforated terrace roof provides a distinctive architectural finish at the upper level.

Together, these elements establish the contemporary character of Villa One while maintaining a clear and composed residential form.
